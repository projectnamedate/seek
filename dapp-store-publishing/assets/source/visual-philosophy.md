# Signal Hunt

Signal Hunt treats the interface as a field camera instrument: exact, dark,
and quietly luminous. Space is not decoration; it is the terrain. Forms should
feel measured, calibrated, and ready for use outside, with camera shutter
irises, circular lens rings, route traces, and target geometry carrying most of
the meaning. The work should look meticulously crafted, with every line weight
and radius chosen by someone at the top of their field.

Color behaves like a live sensor. Black and carbon hold the surface steady,
deep teal creates depth, and frost-cyan light marks the active signal. The
palette should not become a generic crypto gradient or introduce yellow/gold
reward accents into the identity. It should feel like a Seeker-native
operating surface, painstakingly tuned for clarity.

Rhythm comes from repeated instrument marks: aperture blades, rings, ticks,
route points, and subtle grid intervals. These forms should accumulate just
enough detail to make the artifact feel labored over, not generated quickly.
Typography is sparse and functional; the identity is carried by the mark and
composition before words.

The final system should communicate movement from physical search to on-chain
settlement. It should never borrow Solana's logo structure as its own. It can
live near Solana Mobile by sharing restraint, dark surfaces, and cool luminous
materials, while remaining unmistakably Seek.
