### What are you building?

Seek turns real-world spaces into on-chain scavenger hunts. Players stake a
token, receive a physical target, prove completion with a photo, and settle the
result on Solana. The app is already live on Seeker and won the Solana Mobile
Monolith 2026 hackathon.

This Instagrant funds the first Super Hunts milestone: a partner/event mode
where communities can launch geofenced quests, sponsor prize pools, and live
leaderboards. Seek runs on $SKR today, but the Super Hunts layer is being
designed to support partner assets like $BONK, $SOL, or other SPL tokens so
projects can create their own event-based reward flywheels.

### Problem

Crypto events and communities generate attention, but not enough measurable
on-chain action. People take photos, collect swag, and walk past sponsor booths,
but there is usually no fun reason to open a wallet, complete a task, or prove
real engagement.

Super Hunts turns those moments into playable, verifiable quests.

### Why Solana / why Superteam?

Solana is the only chain where this consumer loop feels natural: fast
transactions, cheap settlement, mobile wallet support, active token
communities, and a culture of consumer experimentation. Superteam is the right
first distribution partner because Super Hunts is built for exactly the kind of
community activations, meetups, and founder networks Superteam coordinates.

### What will the grant fund?

The grant funds a scoped 4-week milestone:

1. Event Hunt config schema for geofenced missions, sponsor pools, and
   organizer-owned quest settings.
2. Multi-token architecture for $SKR-first hunts with a path to $SOL, $BONK,
   and partner SPL-token pools.
3. Lightweight organizer playbook for launching a Super Hunt at a meetup,
   conference, or partner booth.
4. Public demo and docs showing how a Solana community could run its own event
   quest.
